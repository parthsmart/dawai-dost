Dawai Dost

Medicine reminders you can talk to.

बोलिये, और दवाई याद रहेगी।

For elderly people in small-town India who find apps hard to type into. Describe your tablets once, out loud, in Hindi, Tamil, Marathi or English. At dose time Dawai Dost speaks up, listens for “kha li”, and tells the family on WhatsApp if a dose is missed.

Try it in a minute

  1. Tap हिंदी and allow the microphone.
  2. Say a medicine: “BP ki safed goli, subah ek, khane ke baad.” Answer “haan” to save it.
  3. Open सेटिंग and tap अगली दवाई के समय पर जाएँ to jump the clock to the dose.
  4. Say “kha li”, or tap +30 min and watch the alert reach परिवार.

Works best in Chrome. No microphone? Every question has a button, and you can type instead.
Speech by Wispr Flow or the browser, understanding by Claude or the built-in parser. Data stays on this device.
